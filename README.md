# ✨ Sparkle Cam

Cute instant-camera photo app with filters, frames, draggable stickers, and sparkle effects. Built for fun!

![Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-online-success)
![HTML5](https://img.shields.io/badge/HTML5-CSS3%20%7C%20JS-blue)

## 📸 Features

- **Live Camera** — tap to start the camera preview
- **Filters** — Normal, Sparkle, Vintage, Pop, Dreamy, B&W
- **Frames** — Polaroid, Cute, Glow, Rainbow, Neon
- **Draggable Stickers** — tap to add, drag to move them around
- **Gallery** — view, download, and delete your photos
- **Saves to Device** — photos are stored locally in your browser
- **Sparkle Effects** — fun animations on every photo

## 🎮 How to Use

1. Open the app on your phone or computer
2. Tap the **pink camera button** 📸 to start
3. Pick a **filter** and **frame**
4. Tap **Stickers** to open the sticker palette, then tap any sticker to add it
5. **Drag stickers** around the screen to position them
6. Tap **camera button** again to take the photo
7. Open **gallery** 🖼️ to see all your photos
8. Download or delete photos anytime
